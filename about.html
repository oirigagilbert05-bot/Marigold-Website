Our story

Started at a kitchen table, in 2019.

Marigold & Co. began as a folding chair and a mirror propped against a kitchen wall, doing friends' hair on Sunday afternoons. Seven years on, we've kept the same unhurried pace — just with better lighting, and a few more chairs.

We believe a good beauty appointment is part skill, part conversation, and part permission to do absolutely nothing for an hour.

What we hold onto

Three things that haven't changed.

Slow beauty

Every appointment has room to breathe — no ten-minute blow-dries, no rushed extractions.

Skilled hands

Every stylist and therapist here trains continuously — technique first, trends second.

A warm welcome

We remember your usual order, your last colour formula, and how your week's been.
